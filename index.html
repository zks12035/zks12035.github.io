Research experience

CFD and machine-learning surrogate for the NACA 63-815 tidal-turbine hydrofoil2026 – presentUndergraduate thesis (NAME 400), BUET · Supervisor: Dr. Mir Tareque Ali
  • Steady RANS (k–ω SST) simulations in OpenFOAM v14 on a 133,200-cell wall-resolved C-grid (y⁺ < 1) for Re = 0.5–8×10⁶ and α = −10° to 18° in 1° steps, automated with Bash and Python scripts (developed with AI assistance).
  • Grid-convergence study; cross-verification against ANSYS Fluent on the same mesh, with lift and cavitation number within 1–2 % in attached flow.
  • Validation of pressures, forces and cavitation inception against the cavitation-tunnel measurements of Molland et al. (2004).
  • Numerical sensitivity of stall prediction (convection schemes, turbulence wall condition, inflow turbulence, solution path, URANS), including two steady RANS solutions past stall that depend on the iteration path.
  • Neural-network surrogate (ensemble of five networks) for lift, drag, moment, stall onset and cavitation inception, with every result carrying its CFD and surrogate uncertainty, and a stall-versus-cavitation design map giving the safe angle of attack for any depth and chord.
  • 133 further pressure-resolved simulations and a reduced-order model (proper orthogonal decomposition with neural networks) that predicts the full surface pressure distribution and where on the foil cavitation starts.
  • Interactive safe-angle tool: enter the angle, Reynolds number (or speed), chord and depth to get the coefficients with uncertainty, the cavitation verdict, the safe angle, the pressure distribution and the computed OpenFOAM flow field (built with AI assistance).
  • The animation above comes from the tool. It is an idealised (potential-flow) picture scaled to the OpenFOAM lift and minimum pressure at Re 2.49 million; move the cursor across it or drag sideways to set the angle between −4° and 16°: orange marks separated flow after stall onset, and bubbles mark where the cavitation criterion σ < σi is met at σ = 4. The RANS model itself is single-phase.
Feasibility of oscillating water column (OWC) wave energy converters for Bangladesh coastal sites2026 – presentWave energy research, BUET · Supervisor: Dr. Zobair Ibn Awal
