Research experience

CFD and machine-learning surrogate for the NACA 63-815 tidal-turbine hydrofoil2026 – presentUndergraduate thesis (NAME 400), BUET · Supervisor: Dr. Mir Tareque Ali
  • Steady RANS (k–ω SST) simulations in OpenFOAM v14 on a 133,200-cell wall-resolved C-grid (y⁺ < 1) for Re = 0.5–8×10⁶ and α = −10° to 18° in 1° steps, automated with Bash and Python scripts (developed with AI assistance).
  • Grid-convergence study; cross-verification against ANSYS Fluent on the same mesh, with lift and cavitation number within 1–2 % in attached flow.
  • Validation of pressures, forces and cavitation inception against the cavitation-tunnel measurements of Molland et al. (2004).
  • Numerical sensitivity of stall prediction: convection schemes, turbulence wall condition, inflow turbulence, solution path and URANS.
  • Neural-network surrogate for lift, drag, stall angle and cavitation inception, and a stall-versus-cavitation design map giving the safe angle of attack for any depth and chord.
  • The video above shows velocity magnitude from these simulations as the angle of attack increases towards stall.
Feasibility of oscillating water column (OWC) wave energy converters for Bangladesh coastal sites2026 – presentWave energy research, BUET · Supervisor: Dr. Zobair Ibn Awal
  • Developed a physics-informed screening model (Gaussian resonance capture efficiency, finite-depth wave power, Wells-turbine and air-compressibility losses) for Cox's Bazar, Saint Martin, Kutubdia and Dublar Char.
  • Showed that the submergence ratio generally recommended in the literature is unfavourable under Bangladesh's long-period monsoon waves; estimated 268 MWh/yr annual energy at Cox's Bazar.
